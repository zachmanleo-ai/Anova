100% Manual Invoice Entry
Every single one of Anova's 900–1,100 monthly invoices is hand-keyed into the ERP. Finding a past invoice means knowing the exact details — and even then it's a pain point for the team.

AP Team Buried in Manual Work
The AP team spends the majority of their time on manual data entry and chasing down invoices. With automation in place, that time can be redirected to higher-value financial work across the organization.

Global Operations Need a Global Tool

Modernization is Overdue
The team wants to modernize and automate — but needs to identify where automation delivers the most value first. AP is the clear starting point. Everyone is aligned, and everyone understands the importance.
📊
Nothing in Place for AP Today
There's no AP tool in place right now. Everything runs on manual processes. Expense is managed through Expensify, though Anova isn't using its full capabilities — a potential consolidation opportunity later.

Step 1: Capture — No More Manual Entry

Invoices arrive from your vendors however they come — email, PDF attachment, scanned paper, or EDI. AI-powered OCR extracts all relevant data automatically: vendor, amount, line items, GL codes, and more. For Anova's ~1,100 monthly invoices, this single step eliminates the bulk of your team's current manual workload across all four entities.

→Email & PDF ingestion
→AI-powered OCR extraction
→Vendor master auto-matching
→GL code suggestion
→Multi-entity intake routing
→Duplicate detection at intake

Step 2: Validate — Smart Matching Without the Headache

Once captured, invoices are automatically validated against POs (Anova uses PO for inventory items), vendor records, and policy rules. Exceptions are flagged for human review; clean invoices move straight through — without anyone touching them. That's the majority of your volume, automatically handled.

→PO matching for inventory
→Policy rule enforcement
→Exception flagging & queuing
→Straight-through processing
→Variance tolerance thresholds
→Approval threshold logic

Step 3: Approve — 40 Approvers, Zero Chaos

Validated invoices are routed automatically to the right approver based on entity, amount, vendor, or any rule Anova defines. Mobile-first approval means no one is stuck at a desk. Auto-reminders keep things moving. For Michelle's team, this means no more manually chasing approvals — the system does it for you.

→Rules-based routing engine
→Mobile approvals anywhere
→Auto-escalation on delays
→Multi-entity approval chains
→Delegation & out-of-office
→Full audit trail per invoice

Step 4: Pay & Post — Closing the Loop

Approved invoices trigger payment and are automatically posted back to your ERP — across all four environments. AP entries are created, the GL is updated, and vendors are paid on schedule. Month-end close gets faster because everything is already reconciled in real time. No manual journal entries, no scrambling at close.

→Multi-ERP posting (all 4)
→Payment initiation
→Multi-currency (USD / EUR)
→Accrual automation
→Vendor payment remittance
→Real-time GL update
